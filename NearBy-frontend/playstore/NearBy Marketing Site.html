Questions

Things people ask before they tap download.

How is NearBy different from Blinkit, Zepto, or Swiggy?

Those are dark stores — warehouses dressed up as shops. NearBy is the actual shop down the street. Same shopkeeper. Same shelf. Same prices. Just delivered.

Are the shops really verified?

Every shop is visited in person before going live. We verify GST, FSSAI (where applicable), and a real human checks the storefront. Trust scores reflect a year of customer feedback.

What if my neighbourhood isn't covered yet?

Drop your pincode in the app and we'll notify you when we onboard a shop in your area. We're adding 80–100 new shops every week.

How does payment work?

UPI, all major cards, and cash on delivery. Refunds within 24 hours. Disputes handled by a real human in the same city — usually the same day.

Are delivery fees fair to riders?

Riders make ₹35–60 per order plus 100% of tips. We commit to a base earning floor per active hour, even on slow days. Numbers are public.
